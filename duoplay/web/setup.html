Joining

The Pi is switching over now. This setup network will disappear and your iPhone will go back to your own Wi-Fi.

Then, in Safari, open
http://duoplay.local:8080
  1. Give it about 30 seconds, then open the address above in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and open DuoPlay from your Home Screen.
The green light on the Pi
On, steady: ready to play. Blinking fast: joining your Wi-Fi. Blinking slowly: it couldn't join. Connect to “DuoPlay Setup” again and check the password.
